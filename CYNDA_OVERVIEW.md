# Cynda Platform Overview

## Departments and Tools

### CRM (Clients)
- **CRM Dashboard**: Overview of customer activity and contact database.
- **Marketing**: Launch and track multi-channel marketing campaigns.
- **Sales Automation**: Automate sales pipeline and lead follow-ups.
- **Sales Reports**: Detailed analytics on conversion rates and revenue.

### Finance
- **Finance Dashboard**: Overview of cash flow, profit, and financial health.
- **Invoicing**: Create professional invoices and track payments.
- **Expenses**: Log business spending and manage reimbursement workflows.
- **Payroll**: Manage employee salaries, taxes, and distributions.
- **Inventory**: Track stock levels, assets, and equipment in real-time.
- **Time Tracking**: Track billable hours and employee time.
- **Payments**: Manage incoming and outgoing payments.
- **Multi-Currency**: Handle transactions in different global currencies.

### Projects
- **Task Management**: Coordinate work across the team with lists and priorities.
- **Kanban Board**: Visual project tracking using boards.
- **List View**: Detailed list-based project management.
- **Calendar View**: Visualize deadlines and milestones on a unified timeline.
- **Timeline / Gantt**: Advanced project planning with dependencies and roadmaps.
- **Resource Planning**: Balance team workload and assign tasks efficiently.

### HR (People)
- **HR Dashboard**: Monitor team health, attendance, and general staff metrics.
- **Directory**: Central hub for all staff contact and profile information.
- **Hiring**: Manage job postings, candidates, and interview pipelines.
- **Onboarding**: Streamline the experience for new team members.
- **Time Off**: Track leave requests, vacations, and sick days.
- **Performance**: Staff performance audits and reviews.
- **HR Analytics**: Workforce analytics and reporting.
- **Surveillance**: Live activity feed and engagement monitoring.

### Other Tools
- **Chat**: Real-time communication with channels and direct messages.
- **Email**: Professional email integration within the workspace.
- **Notes**: Collaborative documents, wikis, and personal knowledge base.
- **Forms**: Build custom forms and databases to collect structured data.
- **Files**: Secure repository for documents and assets.
- **Inbox**: Unified inbox for notifications and messages.

## Automations

### CRM Automations
- **Stale deal follow-up**: Cyndi drafts a follow-up nudge if a deal has had no activity for a set number of days (Gemini Powered).

### Finance Automations
- **Budget threshold alert**: Notifies finance owners if spending approaches or exceeds a configured budget.
- **Overdue invoice reminder**: Drafts a polite payment reminder for clients when an invoice passes its due date (Gemini Powered).

### Project Automations
- **Won deal → project kickoff**: Suggests a project name and kickoff summary when a deal moves to Closed Won (Gemini Powered).

### HR Automations
- **New hire checklist**: Sends onboarding tasks to HR and the hiring manager when a new staff record is added.

### Cross-Department Automations
- **Weekly Monday briefing**: Cyndi summarizes tasks, pipeline, and invoices for leadership every Monday morning (Gemini Powered).
