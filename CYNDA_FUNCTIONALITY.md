# Cynda Project Documentation: Buttons & Functionalities

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the key buttons and functionalities across the Cynda application, organized by the 5 core departments and global settings.

---

## **1. CRM Department**
Manage customer lifecycles, pipelines, and marketing.

### **Dashboard & Global Actions**
- `Data Migration`: (Admin/Dept Head only) Upload and map CSV data to the CRM database.
- `Export`: Download Contacts, Deals, or Companies data as CSV.
- `+ Note`: Quick access to create an untitled note.
- `+ Folder`: Create a new folder for organization.

### **Contacts & Companies**
- `+ Quick Add`: Open dialog to add a new contact or company.
- `Import Logs`: View history of data migrations and imports.

### **Pipeline (Deals)**
- `+ Add Deal`: Create a new deal with value, stage, and probability.
- `Stage Transition`: Drag-and-drop deals through pipeline stages.
- `Closed Won Trigger`: Automatically creates a ghost invoice in Finance and initializes a new project in the Projects module.

### **Marketing & Automation**
- `Create Campaign`: Launch multi-channel marketing campaigns (Running, Scheduled, Draft).
- `Create Workflow`: Define automated triggers (e.g., "Deal Closed Won" -> "Start Email Campaign").
- `Create Report`: Generate analytics for Sales, Marketing, or Team Activity.

---

## **2. Finance Department**
Handle invoicing, expenses, and overall financial health.

### **Invoicing & Payments**
- `New Invoice`: Generate professional invoices for CRM contacts.
- `Let Cyndi handle it`: AI-assisted invoice or expense creation.
- `Record Payment`: Log inbound/outbound transactions (Stripe, Bank Transfer, etc.).

### **Expenses & Payroll**
- `Log Expense`: Categorize and submit business spending for approval.
- `Run Payroll`: (Admin only) Process employee salaries and view paystubs.
- `Add Asset`: Track hardware (e.g., MacBooks) and office equipment in the Inventory tool.

### **Reporting & Tools**
- `Generate Report`: Access P&L statements, Balance Sheets, and Cash Flow trends.
- `Integrations`: Connect to external gateways like Stripe or QuickBooks.
- `Documents`: Securely store financial reports, tax returns, and contracts.

---

## **3. Projects Department**
Coordinate team work with multiple views and resource tracking.

### **Task Management**
- `Add Task`: Create tasks with priorities (High, Medium, Low), due dates, and assignees.
- `Mark Complete`: Move tasks to "Done" and trigger related automations.
- `Subtasks & Comments`: Detailed task-level collaboration.

### **Views & Planning**
- `Board (Kanban)`: Visual stage management for tasks.
- `List View`: Detailed tabular view for quick status audits.
- `Timeline (Gantt)`: Visualize dependencies and roadmaps.
- `Calendar`: Track deadlines on a monthly/weekly timeline.
- `Resource Management`: Balance team workload and assign tasks efficiently.

---

## **4. HR Department**
Manage the global workforce, hiring, and performance.

### **Directory & Org Management**
- `Quick Add`: Add new staff members with role and department assignments.
- `Staff Onboarding`: (Admin only) Initialize the multi-step onboarding flow for new hires.
- `Departments Tool`: Create custom departments and assign specific tool access.

### **Hiring & Performance**
- `Post Job`: Manage job openings, departments, and applicant counts.
- `Review Cycle`: Initiate performance reviews and track employee scores.
- `Surveillance`: (Admin only) Monitor staff activity across the CRM, Finance, and Projects tools.

### **Time & Leave**
- `Clock In/Out`: Track daily working hours.
- `Request Leave`: Submit vacation or sick leave requests for approval.

---

## **5. Communication & Other (Other Dept)**
Centralized tools for collaboration and data collection.

### **Chat**
- `New Group`: Create collaborative group chats with team members.
- `New Channel`: Set up public, department-wide communication channels.
- `New Chat`: Start direct messaging or invite external collaborators.
- `Back Arrow (Mobile)`: Seamlessly switch between chat list and messages on mobile.

### **Inbox & Notifications**
- `Mark All Read`: Clear all unread indicators.
- `Reset All Data`: Developer tool to clear all notifications and project states.
- `Seed Test Data`: Populate the inbox with mock alerts for testing.

### **File Drive**
- `Upload File`: Securely upload documents, images, and archives.
- `File Search`: Quick retrieval of files across all project modules.
- `Manage Access`: Control granular permissions (Owner, Editor, Viewer) for specific team members.
- `Starred Items`: Pin critical documents for quick access in the drive sidebar.

---

## **6. Global Settings & Customization**
Personalize your Cynda experience and manage your workspace.

### **Interface & Theme**
- `Interface Mode`: Switch between Light, Dark, and System modes.
- `Accent Color`: Personalize the primary brand color across the application.
- `Glassmorphism`: Toggle frosted glass effects and blurs for a modern UI.
- `Layout Density`: Switch between Compact, Comfortable, and Spacious information density.
- `Text Scale`: Dynamically adjust font sizes across the entire workspace.

### **Workspace & Billing**
- `Plan Management`: Upgrade/downgrade between Solo, Team, and Organisation tiers.
- `Billing History`: Download receipts and track past subscription payments.
- `Keyboard Shortcuts`: View and manage system-wide hotkeys (e.g., `Cmd + /` for the command panel).

---
*Note: Some features may be restricted based on user role (Super Admin vs. Employee) or subscription tier (Trial vs. Pro).*
